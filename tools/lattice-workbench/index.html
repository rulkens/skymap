This tool runs the lattice simulation on your GPU through WebGPU. It works in Chrome and Edge (desktop and Android), and in Safari 26 or later on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Firefox support is partial.

Gluon Field Workbench

A live SU(2) lattice gauge simulation, running as compute shaders. Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom. Every frame the vacuum is re-sampled, smoothed, and drawn as a volume.
