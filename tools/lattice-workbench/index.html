Gluon Field Workbench

A live lattice gauge simulation running as compute shaders, in the two-colour SU(2) toy or real three-colour SU(3). Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom. The vacuum keeps evolving underneath; smoothing reveals the lumps that persist.

Gauge group
SU(3) links are 3×3 complex matrices: six times the memory and about three times the work per update.
Quarks
Four degenerate staggered flavours, updated by Hybrid Monte Carlo. Lighter quarks cost more solver iterations per step and lower acceptance. Heavy quarks decouple: at m·a ≳ 1 the vacuum is back to gluons only. The T/Tc estimate is for gluons only.
Temperature
Shorter time direction = hotter. Temperature is 1 / (Nt · a).
Larger β = finer lattice spacing a, so also hotter at fixed Nt.
View
0 shows the raw field: pure noise. Smoothing strips short-range fluctuations so the persistent lumps show.
The box is one 3-D slice of a 4-D lattice. Stepping through slices is the classic "boiling vacuum" animation.
Simulation
How much of the field is re-sampled each frame. 0 freezes one configuration; 1 redraws the whole vacuum every frame, so consecutive frames are unrelated. With quarks it sets molecular-dynamics steps per frame instead.
Each frame casts one randomly jittered ray per pixel and blends it into the running image, so a still view sharpens over a second or so. Dragging renders at a third of the resolution.
