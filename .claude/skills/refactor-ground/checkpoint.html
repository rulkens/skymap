The feature needs to report how many black holes it loaded, like every source does.

SelectionKindRow
New optional member
8 readers switch to it
focusId: { encode, decode }
driver?(row): DriverGeometry | null
Why

The feature needs the camera to fly to and orbit the black hole at a sensible distance.

Today a two-arm switch decides what the camera drives around, and its readers look the answer up in the body table. A third arm would make six of them throw.

So each kind of selection answers for its own size.

Weighed

Alternative: put the camera fields on the slab row. Rejected: it grows a render structure with camera data that six readers would then read.
