## What is kept

<Fact id="ref-settings-kept" />

| What | Where it is kept | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orientation, when it is not Ecliptic | In the address, as `orientation=` | As long as the link |
| Every other setting on this page | Nowhere | Until the page is reloaded or closed |
| That you have closed the welcome screen | In the browser’s storage | Until the browser’s data for the site is cleared |

<Fact id="ref-settings-takeover" />

The `orientation` parameter is under [URL parameters](/docs/reference/url-parameters/), and what a link does and does not carry is under [Sharing a view](/docs/guide/sharing/).

## Outside the Settings panel

These are not in the panel, and none of them is kept over a reload either.

| What | Where | Values | At first |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Each panel, folded or open | A click on the panel’s title: Settings or Navigation | Open or folded | Open; folded on a screen narrower than 768 pixels |
| The whole interface, shown or hidden | The Tab key | Shown or hidden | Shown |
| Auto-rotate | The play button in the top bar | On or off | Off |
| The clock: its speed, and running or paused | The clock at the lower right | See [Time](/docs/guide/time/) | Running on the present at 1 s/s |
| Dome mode | `?dome` in the address | On or off | Off |
