A link to skymap is the app’s address with parameters added to it. [Use skymap in the classroom](/classroom/) has links that are ready to hand out, and [Sharing a view](/docs/guide/sharing/) is about copying one from the app. This page is for writing one by hand.

## The parts of an address

```
/?dome#focus=body-saturn&t=2017-10-15T12:00:00Z
```

- `?dome` is a flag. Flags come first, after a `?`, and there is one a visitor needs.
- `#` begins the parameters. Nothing after it is sent to the server: the app reads it in your browser.
- `focus=body-saturn` is one parameter, a name and a value with `=` between them.
- `&` joins one parameter to the next.

## Instants

The value of `t` is an instant.

| Written as | Read as |
| --- | --- |
| `t=2020-12-21T18:00:00Z` | 21 December 2020 at 18:00 UTC |
| `t=2020-12-21T18:00:00+01:00` | 17:00 UTC on that day |
| `t=2020-12-21` | Midnight UTC at the start of that day |
| `t=2020` | Midnight UTC at the start of 1 January 2020 |
| `t=2020-12-21T18:00` | 18:00 on the clock of the computer that opens the link |

## Camera positions

The value of `pose` is an exact camera position.

<Fact id="ref-pose-forms" />

| Form | Values after the letter | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `a` | Seven numbers, or nine | `pose=a,0,0,0,2.1,0.35,260,0` |
| `b` | The name of a body and 15 numbers | Take it from the app |
| `s` | The name of a site and three numbers | `pose=s,perseverance,0.5,0.3,50` |

The seven numbers of the `a` form are, in order:

1. Three for the point the camera turns about, in megaparsecs from the Sun.
2. Two angles for the side the camera looks from, in radians.
3. The distance from that point to the camera, in megaparsecs. It has to be more than zero.
4. The roll of the picture, in radians. Write 0.

The two more that may follow turn the view away from that point, in radians, so that the target sits off-centre.

<Fact id="ref-pose-with-focus" />

The three numbers of the `s` form are the direction the camera stands in and its height above the horizon, both in radians, and its distance from the site in metres, which has to be more than zero. The sites are <Codes of={DOCS_POSE_SITES} />.

The `b` form holds a position and a full set of axes in the body’s own turning frame, in metres. It is what a view close to a surface is written as, and it is not meant to be written by hand.

<Fact id="ref-pose-from-app" />
