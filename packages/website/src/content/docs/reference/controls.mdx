            <td>{row.does}</td>

## Where a control applies

The last column of each table uses these words.

- **Anywhere** is the scene, a tour, an exhibit and the welcome screen, as long as no text field has the keyboard.
- **The scene and exhibits** leaves tours out. <Fact id="tour-no-steering" inline />
- **A text field** is the search field or the date field of the clock.

## What has no control

- **Flying with the keyboard.** No key moves the camera by itself: the keys choose a target, and the mouse, the trackpad or a finger does the rest.
- **Auto-rotate.** <Fact id="auto-rotate" inline />
- **Sliding the view and tilting it, on a touch screen.** Both need the right or the middle mouse button.
- **The buttons, from the keyboard.** <Keys of="Tab" /> does not reach them, as said above.
