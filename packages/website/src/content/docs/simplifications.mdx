## What not to claim in class

- That the map shows the universe at one moment: see [Time and motion](#time-and-motion).
- That a streak of galaxies pointing at the Sun is a real shape: see [Distances and redshift space](#distances-and-redshift-space).
- That a dark gap, or a region with fewer points, is empty space: see [Survey selection and completeness](#survey-selection-and-completeness).
- That two points of equal brightness give off equal light, or that the colours are what an eye would see: see [Brightness, exposure and colour](#brightness-exposure-and-colour).
- That a dot’s size is the size of the thing: see [Sizes](#sizes).
- That the planets stand where the app puts them in any year you choose, or that Earth’s clouds are today’s weather: see [The Solar System](#the-solar-system) and [Time and motion](#time-and-motion).
- That the Milky Way seen from outside is a photograph, or the black hole a picture taken by a telescope: see [The Milky Way](#the-milky-way).
- That the blue sphere is a wall, or anything a telescope has seen: see [Structures](#structures).

## What is missing altogether

<Fact id="scene-not-here" />

<Fact id="scene-rings" />

Beyond the Solar System the scene holds only what a catalogue we read holds. A galaxy too faint for those surveys is not in the map, wherever it is. No nebula and no star cluster is an object of its own, though a cluster’s stars are there one by one where Gaia has them.

If you find a departure that is not on this page, <a href={`${REPO_URL}/issues`}>open an issue in the repository</a>.
