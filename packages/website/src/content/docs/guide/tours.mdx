skymap has two tours.

## The Long Way Out

“The Long Way Out” has 14 captioned steps, from the Milky Way to the edge of the observable universe and back. Left to play, it takes a little over 5 minutes: the shortest step takes 7 seconds and the longest 41.

## Start a tour

| | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Mouse or trackpad | Open the search, choose the Tours tab and click a card |
| Touch screen | Tap the search box, choose the Tours tab and tap a card |
| Keyboard | <Keys of="/" />, type the tour’s name, <Keys of="Enter" /> |

The Tour button on the welcome screen starts “The Long Way Out”, and the About button in the top bar brings the welcome screen back.

## Pause, step and leave

| | Next step | Previous step | Pause or resume | Leave |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mouse, trackpad or touch screen | The right arrow at the foot | The left arrow at the foot | The round button with two bars | The round button with a square |
| Keyboard | <Keys of="Right" /> | <Keys of="Left" /> | <Keys of="Space" /> | <Keys of="Esc" /> |

A tour pauses only once the camera has arrived at a step’s subject. The ring round the pause button runs down as the step’s time runs out, and a paused step keeps its caption until you resume it or step past it.

While a tour plays, dragging and zooming do nothing. While it is paused they move the camera as usual, and the next step takes the camera on to its own subject. The panels, the search box and the clock are hidden, though <Keys of="/" /> still opens the search. The clock keeps running and its keys still work: see [Time](/docs/guide/time/).

A tour ends after its last step, or when you press <Keys of="Right" /> on that step, or when you leave it. The panels come back, the layers the tour switched are put back as they were, and the camera stays where the tour left it. Press <Keys of="H" /> to come back to Earth.

If a tour ends early, start it again from the search.

## Captions and the column of dots

<Fact id="tour-caption" />

A column of dots at the right edge has one dot for each step, with the current one lit. Pointing at a dot shows that step’s heading.

## Open and leave an exhibit

| | Open | Leave |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mouse or trackpad | Open the search and click the exhibit’s card | The Exit view button at the foot |
| Touch screen | Tap the search box and tap the exhibit’s card | The Exit view button at the foot |
| Keyboard | <Keys of="/" />, type its name, <Keys of="Enter" /> | <Keys of="Esc" /> |

<Fact id="exhibit-steering" />

<Figure id="dome-exhibit" />

Leaving puts the layers back as you had them and leaves the camera where it is. Opening another exhibit from the search leaves the first.

The clock is hidden in an exhibit too, and its keys work there as well: in the Solar System exhibit, press <Keys of="]" /> a few times and the planets move along their orbits.

## A tour or an exhibit in a link

While a tour or an exhibit is open the address names it, and a link with that address opens straight into it, without the welcome screen. A tour always starts at its first step. An exhibit link can carry a date as well.

```
#tour=grandTour
#exhibit=solarSystem&t=2020-12-21T18:00:00Z
```

[Sharing a view](/docs/guide/sharing/) has the rest of the address, and the [Object catalogue](/docs/reference/objects/#exhibits) lists the name of each tour and exhibit.
