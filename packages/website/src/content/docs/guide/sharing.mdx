## The address is the link

Everything after the `#` is written by the app. The address with nothing after it is the home view: Earth, at the present moment.

```
#focus=body-saturn&t=2033-03-14T21:00:00.000Z
```

The parts are joined with `&`. Each part (a parameter, in the reference) is in a table on [URL parameters](/docs/reference/url-parameters/).

To move the focus to a pinned object, press <Keys of="F" /> or the Focus button on its card. <Keys of="Esc" /> takes the focus out of the address. See [Moving around](/docs/guide/moving/).

### The date and time

Until you set a date or change the clock’s speed the address holds no date, even with the clock paused, and the link opens on the present of whoever opens it. Set a date, and the link opens on that date. [Time](/docs/guide/time/) says when the app writes `t` and which forms of date it reads.

## Make a link

1. Find the object: open the search with <Keys of="/" />, type its name and press <Keys of="Enter" />.
2. If the date matters, set it: click the date on the clock at the lower right, type a date and a time in UTC, and press <Keys of="Enter" />. The clock pauses there.
3. Copy the address from the address bar. On a phone, use the browser’s own share button.
4. Open the link in a new window before the lesson, on the screen you will use, and look at what it shows.

## What the person who opens it sees

<Fact id="share-opens" />

Two people who open the same link read the same digits on the clock wherever they are. To let the scene run, press <Keys of="\" />; to return to the present, <Keys of="Shift+N" />.

What differs is the receiver’s own screen.

- **The frame.** A wider or narrower window shows more or less of the scene around the object.
- **The data size.** A phone starts with the smallest data size; see [Settings](/docs/guide/settings/#see-more-or-fewer-galaxies). A planet, or a named galaxy such as Andromeda, opens at any size. <Fact id="share-data-size" inline />
- **The card.** On a phone the card is a sheet at the foot of the screen; see [Screens, quality and domes](/docs/guide/screens-and-domes/#phones-and-tablets).

<Fact id="share-unknown" />

## What a link does not carry

<Fact id="share-settings" />

If a lesson needs the names off, a catalogue on or another data size, say so beside the link, or set it on the room’s computer before the class comes in and leave the page open.

<Fact id="share-place" />

The 18 places are listed under [Finding things](/docs/guide/finding/).

## Tours, exhibits and dome mode

A link can start a tour or open an exhibit, as in `#tour=webShowcase` and `#exhibit=solarSystem`, and an exhibit’s link can hold a date as well. See [Tours and exhibits](/docs/guide/tours/).

<Fact id="share-tour-focus" />

For a planetarium, `?dome` goes before the `#`, and the app keeps it there while it rewrites the rest. See [Screens, quality and domes](/docs/guide/screens-and-domes/#dome-mode).
