## What you are looking at

Four things on screen answer this.

- **The ring.** <Fact id="selection-ring" inline />
- **The card.** Its first line gives the kind of object: a body of the solar system, a star, a galaxy or a structure. Its heading gives the name.
- **The scale bar.** <Fact id="scale-bar" inline /> The bar holds at that distance only: something nearer that looks as long as the bar is shorter than it says, and something further away is longer.
- **The address.** <Fact id="lesson-link" inline />
