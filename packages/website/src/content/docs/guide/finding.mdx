## Open the search

Press <Keys of="/" />, or <Keys of="Ctrl+K" /> (<Keys of="Cmd+K" /> on a Mac), or click the search box at the top.

## Before you type

Before you type, the search shows 75 picture cards in seven tabs. A card flies to an object, opens an exhibit or starts a tour. Pointing at a card shows a line about it and the other names it goes by.

## Type a name

<Fact id="search-scope" />

<Figure id="guide-search-typed" />

The search ignores capitals, and the best match is first. Named galaxies and structures are also found by a word in their description. If a galaxy you want is not listed, type more of its name.

Choosing a result selects the object and flies the camera to it. Type the name of a tour or an exhibit to open it: see [Tours and exhibits](/docs/guide/tours/). The keys that work inside the search are in [Controls](/docs/reference/controls/).

## Catalogue numbers and other names

<Fact id="search-catalogue-galaxies" />

For these galaxies the space may be left out: “ugc12889” finds UGC 12889. A named galaxy is matched as it is written: type “M31” without a space and “NGC 224” with one.

A named star is also found by its Bayer designation, a Greek letter with the constellation, and by its numbers in the HD and Hipparcos (HIP) catalogues: “Alpha Canis Majoris” finds Sirius. Spacecraft answer to their short names too: “Percy” finds Perseverance.

Every named object is listed in the [Object catalogue](/docs/reference/objects/).

## Click and point in the scene

| | See its name | Pin its card | Unpin it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mouse or trackpad | Rest the pointer on the object | Click it | Click empty space or the cross |
| Touch screen | No control | Tap it | Tap empty space or the cross |
| Keyboard | No control | No control | <Keys of="Esc" /> |

A click pins the card and leaves the view where it is. A pinned card is marked “Pinned”, and carries a Focus button and the cross that unpins it. A click on a name in the scene selects the object it names.

<Figure id="class-card" />

Near Earth a click on the sky usually lands on a star and selects it, so use the cross or <Keys of="Esc" /> there. On a phone the card is a sheet at the foot of the screen: drag it up by the bar at its top to reach the cross.

What a card lists for each kind of object is in [Info cards](/docs/guide/info-cards/). To send the camera to what you have selected, see [Go somewhere else](/docs/guide/moving/#go-somewhere-else).

## What you are looking at

Four things on screen answer this.

- **The ring.** <Fact id="selection-ring" inline />
- **The card.** Its first line gives the kind of object: a body of the solar system, a star, a galaxy or a structure. Its heading gives the name.
- **The scale bar.** <Fact id="scale-bar" inline /> The bar holds at that distance only: something nearer that looks as long as the bar is shorter than it says, and something further away is longer.
- **The address.** It names the object the camera is on: see [Sharing a view](/docs/guide/sharing/).

## Names in the scene

Each kind of name has a switch under Labels & Guides in the Settings panel: see [Settings](/docs/guide/settings/).

## What the search does not find

<Fact id="unnamed-stars" />

<Fact id="constellations-not-findable" />

The Zone of Avoidance band can be selected, but it is a direction on the sky, so the camera cannot fly to it.

In dome mode nothing can be clicked, so use the search: see [Screens, quality and domes](/docs/guide/screens-and-domes/).

The search holds only what the scene holds. [What is in the scene](/docs/start/scene/) lists it.
