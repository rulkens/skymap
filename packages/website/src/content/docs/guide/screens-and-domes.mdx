## Phones and tablets

A phone starts with the smallest data size; see [Settings](/docs/guide/settings/#see-more-or-fewer-galaxies).

<Fact id="card-phone-sheet" />

<Fact id="settings-phone" />

Two fingers zoom and do nothing else; [Moving around](/docs/guide/moving/) has the gestures. A phone has no <Keys of="Tab" /> key, so the interface cannot be hidden.

<Fact id="screens-tablet" />

<Fact id="screens-size-once" />

To load more on a phone that can take it, open the Settings panel and set Tier to Medium.

## Laptops and desktops

A window wider than 768 pixels opens with the medium data size, both panels at the lower left and the card at the upper right. Every key and mouse action is in [Controls](/docs/reference/controls/).

<Fact id="screens-draws-on-change" />

[Auto-rotate](/docs/guide/moving/#look-around-an-object), and [a clock set faster than real time](/docs/guide/time/#run-pause-and-change-speed), keep it drawing.

## Big screens and projectors

<Fact id="screens-no-fullscreen" />

That is <Keys of="F11" /> on Windows and Linux, and on a Mac <Keys of="Ctrl+Cmd+F" /> or the green button of the window.

<Fact id="screens-interface-size" />

The browser’s zoom, <Keys of="Ctrl" /> or <Keys of="Cmd" /> with the plus key, enlarges the panels, the cards and the search.

For a dark room:

1. Open the view you want and make the window fill the screen.
2. Under Display, lower [Exposure](/docs/reference/glossary/#exposure) by one or two EV. A picture set for a lit office is harsh on a wall in the dark.
3. Turn off the names you do not need, or all of them with the switch on Labels & Guides.
4. Press <Keys of="Tab" /> to hide the interface. The clock’s keys and <Keys of="H" /> for Home still work with it hidden. Press <Keys of="Tab" /> again before you search: the search is part of the interface.

No setting survives a reload, so set them once the page is open and leave it open. See [Settings](/docs/guide/settings/#make-it-brighter-or-calmer).

## When it stutters

<Fact id="screens-no-quality-setting" />

Try them in order and stop when the picture moves smoothly.

1. **Choose a smaller data size.** Set Tier, in the title of the Settings panel, to Medium or Small. The app then draws fewer points and holds less in the graphics processor’s memory.
2. **Make the window smaller.** The work for a frame grows with the number of pixels, and a window half as wide and half as high has a quarter of them.
3. **Switch layers off.** [The glow of the cosmic web](/docs/rendering/techniques/#the-cosmic-web), the filaments, the flow ribbons and [Bloom](/docs/rendering/techniques/#bloom-tone-curves-and-hdr) are each drawn in a step of their own, which a switch leaves out. A catalogue of galaxies that is switched off is left out too.
4. **Raise Detail**, under Stars and then Advanced. At a higher value fewer of the far stars are drawn one by one.
5. **Close what else is using the graphics processor**: other tabs with video or 3D, and other programs.

[Dome mode](/docs/rendering/#the-dome) costs more than a flat view of the same window. If the app does not start at all, or stops with a message, see [Browser support and troubleshooting](/docs/start/browsers/#when-the-app-does-not-start).
