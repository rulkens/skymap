## Big screens and projectors

<Fact id="screens-no-fullscreen" />

That is <Keys of="F11" /> on Windows and Linux, and on a Mac <Keys of="Ctrl+Cmd+F" /> or the green button of the window.

<Fact id="screens-interface-size" />

The browser’s zoom, <Keys of="Ctrl" /> or <Keys of="Cmd" /> with the plus key, enlarges the panels, the cards and the search.

For a dark room:

1. Open the view you want and make the window fill the screen.
2. Under Display, lower Exposure by one or two EV. A picture set for a lit office is harsh on a wall in the dark.
3. Turn off the names you do not need, or all of them with the switch on Labels & Guides.
4. Press <Keys of="Tab" /> to hide the interface. The clock’s keys and <Keys of="H" /> for Home still work with it hidden. Press <Keys of="Tab" /> again before you search: the search is part of the interface.

No setting survives a reload, so set them once the page is open and leave it open. See [Settings](/docs/guide/settings/#make-it-brighter-or-calmer).

## When it stutters

<Fact id="screens-no-quality-setting" />

We have not measured how much each of these steps gains on any machine. Each takes away work in the way it says, so try them in order and stop when the picture moves smoothly.

1. **Choose a smaller data size.** Set Tier, in the title of the Settings panel, to Medium or Small. The app then draws fewer points and holds less in the graphics processor’s memory.
2. **Make the window smaller.** The work for a frame grows with the number of pixels, and a window half as wide and half as high has a quarter of them.
3. **Switch layers off.** The glow of the cosmic web, the filaments, the flow ribbons and Bloom are each drawn in a step of their own, which a switch leaves out. A catalogue of galaxies that is switched off is not drawn either.
4. **Raise Detail**, under Stars and then Advanced. At a higher value fewer of the far stars are drawn one by one.
5. **Close what else is using the graphics processor**: other tabs with video or 3D, and other programs.

Dome mode costs more than a flat view of the same window. If the app does not start at all, or stops with a message, see [Browser support and troubleshooting](/docs/start/browsers/#when-the-app-does-not-start).

### Live or as a film

<Fact id="screens-no-capture" />

- **Live.** Show the browser window, full screen and with the interface hidden, on the output your system takes a fisheye from. Someone flies the app while the audience watches.
- **As a film.** The recorder writes a fisheye disc of 4,096 by 4,096 pixels at 30 or 60 frames a second, without sound.

What we would build for a dome, and what is still missing for one, is on [Domes and museums](/domes/#deliver).
