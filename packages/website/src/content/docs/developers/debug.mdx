## Flags

A flag goes after a `?` in the address, before the `#`. <Fact id="ref-params-query" inline />

<Table
  cols="detail"
  head={['Flag', 'What it does', 'Skips the welcome screen']}

<Example to="pose=a,0,0,0,2.1,0.35,260,0" query="gpuTimings" address label="Open this view with timings">

After the `#`, `clip` plays one flight of the camera with no captions and no steps: `clip=sondermarkenFlyout` is the flight the home page of this site opens. The flag `dome` and every other parameter are in [URL parameters](/docs/reference/url-parameters/).

## Timings

<Fact id="debug-timings" />

Each row under GPU Timings is one drawing step of the frame, grouped as the frame is drawn; [The frame](/docs/rendering/) says what the steps are. The same numbers, taken at fixed views and written to a table, are what [`npm run perf`](/docs/developers/cli/#measuring) reports.
