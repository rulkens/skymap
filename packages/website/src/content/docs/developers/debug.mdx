## A link that holds the camera

The “copy URL” button under Camera copies a link that holds the camera as well, and <Keys of="L" /> prints the same link to the browser’s console. [Sharing a view](/docs/guide/sharing/#what-the-app-writes-and-when) says what it holds, and [Camera positions](/docs/reference/url-parameters/#camera-positions) what the numbers of a pose mean.
