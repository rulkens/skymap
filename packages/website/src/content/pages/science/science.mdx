<Section id="sources">
  <SectionHead title="The sources">
    <p>This is every dataset Skymap is built from: the catalogues of stars and galaxies, the orbits, the named structures and the imagery. We list them so that anything you see in the app can be traced to who measured it. Each name opens a page with its licence and the files we use.</p>

  <SourceTable>
    <p>{fact('sci-built-counts').text}{' '}<a href={fact('sci-built-counts').source}>{fact('sci-built-counts').sourceLabel}</a></p>
    <p>The count is what the app draws. It is smaller than the source’s own total because rows without a usable <a href={sitePath('/docs/reference/glossary/#redshift')}>redshift</a> are dropped, <a href={sitePath('/docs/data/pipeline/#cross-match-and-de-duplicate')}>overlapping catalogues are merged</a>, and <a href={sitePath('/docs/data/sdss/')}>SDSS</a> is cut to about 500,000 of its most luminous galaxies. Where a count is missing, neither our build nor the source’s own record gives one.</p>
    <p>Terrain heights, the Mars rover sites, 3D models, the constellation lines, the photographs of named galaxies and the survey pictures fetched for other galaxies are not in the table. They are recorded in the repository’s <a href={`${REPO_URL}/tree/main/data/raw`}>data records</a> and its{' '}<a href={`${REPO_URL}/blob/main/ATTRIBUTIONS.md`}>attributions file</a>.</p>
