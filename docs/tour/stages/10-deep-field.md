## Intent

Push past the local universe into the deep field — the quasar population
shells out toward the horizon. Everything local collapses to a glowing core.

## Camera

A big, near-continuous log-dolly (~150 → ~2,000 Mpc) centred on the origin —
no settle-orbit, the motion itself is the beat. Short dwell.

## On screen

`The deep field` + narration. No structure label here; the quasars carry it.

## Effects

- Ensure the milliquas source is visible and, if needed, nudge its
  brightness so the shell reads against the thinning field.

## Tweaks

- Focus is the origin as an arbitrary point — there's no single object to
  frame at this scale.
