## Intent

The *emptiness* third of the cosmic-web triad (05 web → 06 flows → 07 voids).
After the dense threads and the flows that feed them, the complement: the
voids — where the matter drains *from*. The flows (06) are the bridge that
makes the **dichotomy** causal: fullness and emptiness are two ends of the
same currents. Mid-sequence on purpose: the camera never *ends* on a void.

## Camera

A lateral drift at roughly held scale — the focus slides across the same
field to centre a void, little change in `logDist`. The emptiness is felt by
the slide *within the web*, not by zooming into nothing.

## On screen

`The emptiness` + narration; the narration explicitly calls back to stage 05
("the empty side of that web"). The Boötes Void name auto-labels — and
reassures the viewer the blank region is the subject, not a glitch.

## Effects

- The MCPM volume + filaments (05) **stay on** — the void must read as a
  *dark region of the same density field*, not an unlit gap. The flow field
  (06) can ease back here so the void reads clean, or stay faint to show the
  current draining outward. Either way the pairing stays legible in one web.

## Tweaks

- Don't dwell too long; 5 s is enough to register the contrast against 05.
- Frame so a bright filament/node is still at the edge of shot — the void
  reads as empty only *next to* fullness.
