# More binary-star orbits

Alpha Centauri B became the first famous star positioned by its orbit on 2026-10-28
([spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-binary-star-orbits-design.md)). Any famous-star seed row may
now carry an `orbit` around another row, relative to its host, in published visual-binary
elements.

## Candidates

- **Sirius B**, the white dwarf: P 50.1 yr, a 7.5″. Bond et al. 2017 (ApJ 840, 70).
- **Procyon B**, also a white dwarf: P 40.8 yr. Bond et al. 2015.
- **61 Cygni A/B:** P ≈ 680 yr, poorly constrained.
- **Proxima around Alpha Centauri AB:** P ≈ 550 kyr (Kervella et al. 2017). It needs AB's barycentre
  as the host (see `2026-08-16-barycentric-orbit-pairs.md`).

## Verification gap

The Alpha Centauri tests pin B's 3D distances and its 2016 apparent separation (about 4″). Neither
can see Ω, which only turns the pair on the sky. One position angle from the USNO ORB6 ephemeris
at a stated epoch would close that gap, and each new binary should carry one.
