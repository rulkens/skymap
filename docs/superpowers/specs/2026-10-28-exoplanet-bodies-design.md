# Exoplanets as scene bodies (TRAPPIST-1 pilot)

Status: converged 2026-10-28 in session; ground preparation below.

## 1. Intent

The exoplanet-system prototype drew each system's orbits with a renderer of its own: face-on rings,
then an orbit-plane shader. The app already draws orbits in 3D (`orbitTrailRenderer`), planets as
lit spheres and glints (`planetsPass`, `bodyGlintsPass`), and puts bodies on the sim clock
(`deriveBodyStates`). The S-stars show these work far from the Sun. TRAPPIST-1 becomes the first
system drawn with them: a real host star, seven planets on their measured orbits, and 3D trails. The
custom orbit renderer is deleted.

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals**

- TRAPPIST-1 is a scene star, and its seven planets are scene planets: searchable, focusable
  (`#focus=body-trappist-1-e`), captioned, and placed on the sim clock.
- Their orbits are drawn by the orbit-trail renderer, in the system's measured plane, and toggled by
  the existing "Orbit trails" switch.
- Each planet is lit by its own star, not the Sun.
- `#focus=exoplanet-system-trappist-1` still frames the whole system.

**Non-goals**

- The other 18 exoplanet systems. They keep the face-on diagram until they migrate (backlog item,
  plan task 6). Each needs its host as a scene star first.
- The habitable zone. Trails exist only for bodies; the green band is dropped for TRAPPIST-1.
- Textures or measured albedos for the planets. JWST albedos exist only for b and c; the planets get
  one dark rocky albedo.

**Anti-goals**

- A Layer drawing bodies another Layer owns (the body-layers spec's anti-goal). The planets join the
  planet table, not the structure Layer.
- A second orbit renderer. The orbit-plane renderer is deleted, not kept beside the trails.
- A per-system special case in lighting (`if trappist …`).

## 3. Design

### 3.1 Host star

TRAPPIST-1 is a `famous_stars.seed.json` row, generated by `npm run build-famous-stars`, the way
Proxima Centauri is. That gives it an anchor (`SCENE_ANCHORS`) and a `StarBody`. The body-layers
spec moves all seeded stars to starCatalog later (its §7 step 5); this row moves with them.

Facts (NASA Exoplanet Archive and SIMBAD): RA 346.6264°, Dec −5.0435°, 12.43 pc, V 18.8,
M_V 18.4, M8V, R 0.1192 R☉, T 2566 K, M 0.0898 M☉, L 0.000553 L☉, age 7.6 Gyr,
Gaia DR3 2635476908753563008.

### 3.2 Planets

`src/data/bodies/exoplanetPlanets.ts` holds the seven seeds, one per planet, in published units:
semi-major axis (au), period (d), eccentricity, sky inclination (°), transit midpoint (BJD) and
radius (R⊕), all from the archive's `pscomppars`. A maker `exoplanet(seed)` turns each seed into a
`PlanetBody` and an `OrbitalElements` row. The rows are spread into `SCENE_PLANETS` and
`ORBITAL_ELEMENTS`, beside the solar planets. The body PR later moves them into the `body` Layer.

The orbital frame is the sky plane at the host, `planeFrameFromPole(ra, dec)`, as the S-stars do it.
Inclination converts with `skyInclinationToFrameInclination`. The node's position angle is unknown
for transiting planets and is set to 0 by convention (§5). The mean anomaly at J2000 comes from the
transit midpoint, where the planet sits between its star and the Sun.

### 3.3 Trails

The planets' rows land in `ORBITAL_ELEMENTS`, so they join `CORE_TRAIL_ELEMENTS` and the "Orbit
trails" group with no new show key. No new toggle is needed: these are planets' orbits.

### 3.4 Structure anchor

The `exoplanet-system-trappist-1` anchor keeps its ring, card and focus link. Its seed radius stays
the system's extent (planet h, 0.06189 au), so the focus frames the trails. TRAPPIST-1 leaves
`EXOPLANET_ORBITS`, so the face-on diagram never draws over the bodies. The seed-radius test reads
the extent from the planet rows for a host that has them.

## 4. Ground preparation

The refactor-ground pass asked what a body orbiting a star other than the Sun hits in today's code.
There are two joints. Both are fixed as their own commits ahead of the feature.

**G1. Light comes from the body's root anchor, not the render origin.** Eight passes call
`sunDirLocal(bodyPos, RENDER_ORIGIN_MPC, orientation)`: planets, textured bodies, rings, earth,
cloud shell, surface tiles, atmosphere, mesh bodies. The glint phase (`bodyGlintBrightness`) reads
`renderOriginMpc` too. A TRAPPIST-1 planet would be lit from Earth's side.

From-scratch shape: a body's light source is the root of its focus graph, the walk `bodyHostId`
already defines (the Sun for the solar system, the host star for an exoplanet). Changes:
- `rootIdOf` becomes `src/utils/scene/rootBodyIdOf.ts`, extracted from `bodyRegions.ts:27`.
- A new helper reads the root's position from the snapshot.
- `sunDirLocal` is renamed `lightDirLocal` with `npm run refactor`, since it no longer always points
  at the Sun.
- Every caller passes the root's position, and the glint phase takes the same position.

The Sun's anchor sits at the render origin, so every solar-system body is lit exactly as today.
**Verdict: growth.** One rule replaces an assumption; no branch per system.

**G2. Bodies rooted at an unclaimed star join the neighbourhood region.** `bodyRegions.ts` puts the
star anchors themselves in `solar-neighbourhood`, but a body orbiting one of those stars falls into
no region. That breaks the "total + disjoint over `SCENE_BODIES`" invariant `BodyRegion` states.
From-scratch shape: the neighbourhood is everything rooted at an unclaimed anchor. **Verdict:
growth.**

**Checked, no prep needed:**
- **Planet table:** a non-mesh body joins `SCENE_PLANETS` directly.
- **Trail roster:** orbital rows join core trails on their own.
- **Slab precision:** body slabs are built per body in f64. The S-stars already draw at 8 kpc.
- **Foreground gate:** `FOREGROUND_MAX_DISTANCE_MPC` already spans the neighbourhood.

## 5. Rulings

1. **The node angle is a convention.** Inclination fixes the plane's tilt to our sky. Its spin about
   the line of sight (Ω) is unmeasured for transiting planets, so it is drawn at PA 0, north. The
   detail card text must not claim more.
2. **One albedo for all seven:** a dark rocky grey, linear [0.2, 0.19, 0.18]. JWST thermal
   phase curves put b and c at low albedo, and the rest are unmeasured.
3. **The orbit-plane renderer is deleted.** The other 18 systems fall back to the face-on diagram
   from commit `2672033` until they migrate. A renderer kept for 18 systems while bodies serve one
   would be two orbit paths standing side by side.
4. **The habitable-zone band is dropped** for TRAPPIST-1 in this pilot. A trail-renderer annulus is
   a separate decision.

## 6. Risks

- **Two search rows:** "TRAPPIST-1" lists both the star and the exoplanet-system anchor. They are
  different targets (the star body and the featured system), so both stay. This is noted, not fixed.
- **The planets render as glints at system scale.** Earth-sized planets are under a pixel at the
  framing distance. The trails and captions carry the view, and a planet resolves into a sphere on
  its own focus.
- **Gaia dedup:** TRAPPIST-1 (G ≈ 15.6) may already be a Gaia catalogue point. The star-catalog
  dedup reads `gaiaDr3` from the famous-star table at build time, so the hosted chunks keep a
  duplicate point until the next chunk re-bake.

## 7. Packaging

G1 and G2 are prep commits ahead of the feature commits. Whether they ride this branch or go up as
their own PR is asked at the checkpoint.

## 8. Success criteria

- `#focus=exoplanet-system-trappist-1` shows the star and seven elliptical trails in perspective, with
  each planet's glint on its trail.
- `#focus=body-trappist-1-e` frames a lit sphere whose lit side faces TRAPPIST-1.
- At a transit midpoint the planet sits between its star and the Sun (a test).
- Turning off "Orbit trails" hides the TRAPPIST-1 trails along with the solar ones.
- The orbit-plane renderer, its shader and its types are gone. The other 18 systems draw the face-on
  diagram.
