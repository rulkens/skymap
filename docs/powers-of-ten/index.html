Powers of Ten

centered on you — zoom out to the cosmos, in to your atoms
Show status

This ladder is centered on you. At 10⁰ m a 3D figure of the visitor stands at their real geolocation on Earth, head tilted back, looking up — the pivot of the whole journey. Zoom out and you rise through your city, the planet, the solar system, into the cosmic web skymap already renders. Zoom in and you enter the eye that was doing the looking: through the retina, into one nerve cell whose axon carries the sky to your brain, into that cell's nucleus, down to the DNA, one carbon atom, and the quarks within. The organ that looks out is the way in — the Eames descent, made personal and closed into a loop.
