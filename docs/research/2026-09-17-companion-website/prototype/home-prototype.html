What is measured, what is drawn

Measured

  • Star positions and colours, from Gaia
  • Galaxy positions, from SDSS, 2MRS and GLADE
  • Planet and moon orbits, from JPL
  • Earth's surface, from satellite imagery

Drawn from models

  • The Milky Way seen from outside
  • Atmospheres and how they scatter light
  • The filaments traced between galaxies
